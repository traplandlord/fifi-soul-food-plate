# Fifi's Soul Food Plate — Business Plan (Practical Draft)

**Brand:** Fifi's Soul Food Plate (also Fifi Soul Food)  
**Model:** Home-cooked, packaged hot plates sold from car, farmers markets, trade shows — **not** a full restaurant  
**Assumed market:** Southern California / LA area (America/Los_Angeles)  
**Draft date:** Sep 22, 2026  
**Status:** Planning draft — costs sourced or clearly marked as estimates (see `cost-sheet.md`)

---

## 1. Concept

Home-style soul food plated and held hot for cash / card sales at markets, events, and car-side pop-ups. One locked core plate keeps prep simple, quality consistent, and food cost predictable.

**Promise:** Hot fried chicken, collard greens, and cornbread — cooked like home, sold ready to eat.

## 2. Menu (v1 locked)

| Item | Spec | Notes |
|------|------|--------|
| **Core plate** | Fried chicken (2–3 pieces) + collard greens (~4–6 oz cooked) + cornbread (1 square or muffin) | Do not change for first 4–8 weeks |
| Optional later | Mashed potatoes as alternate/add side | After core plate is stable |
| Optional later | Drinks: coffee, hot chocolate, agua frescas | Separate margin; needs beverage handling rules |

**Piece suggestion per plate:** 2 drumsticks **or** 1 drumstick + 1–2 wings (≈0.55–0.80 lb raw chicken depending on size). See cost sheet for $/plate impact.

**Target sell price (v1):** **$14–16** (recommended start **$15**). Market comps for LA soul-food chicken plates often land ~$18–25 at brick-and-mortar; mobile/market can sit slightly under that and still clear a strong margin. Details in `cost-sheet.md`.

## 3. Operations flow

```
PLAN BATCH → BUY (Depot / grocery) → COOK AT APPROVED KITCHEN
    → PORTION & PACKAGE → HOLD HOT (insulated bags / chafer / Cambro-style)
    → TRANSPORT → SELL (market / car / event) → CLEANDOWN & LOGS
```

### A. Cook (home / MEHKO kitchen)
1. Season and fry chicken in batches; drain; hold above safe hot temps.
2. Cook collards (fresh preferred for brand; canned/frozen OK for backup / yield).
3. Bake cornbread (mix for speed, or scratch when volume is steady).
4. Cool only if needed for early prep; otherwise go straight to hot-hold packaging.

### B. Package
- 3-compartment hinged container **or** foil pan + lid (keeps grease and moisture manageable).
- Portion greens with ladle (consistent 4–6 oz).
- Place cornbread in dry compartment or wrap to limit sogginess.
- Add utensil kit + napkin + wet wipe.

### C. Hold heat (car / booth)
- Insulated delivery bags (Uline-style) for short hops.
- For markets/events: chafer with fuel **or** insulated pan carrier; probe thermometer in the hot-hold unit.
- Target: keep hot food **≥135°F** (standard hot-holding rule of thumb — confirm with your local Environmental Health inspector).
- Do **not** rely on closed car heat alone for multi-hour holds.

### D. Sell
- Farmers markets, trade shows, pre-announced car pop-ups (parking rules + permit first).
- Cash + Square/Toast-style card reader.
- Simple ticket: plate name, price, allergens (wheat, egg, dairy if used), reheating note if any.

### E. Cleandown
- Oil filtration / discard schedule, sanitizer logs, leftover policy (discard after service window — no unsafe re-service).

## 4. Legal / permit path (high-level only — not legal advice)

**Critical:** California **Cottage Food Operations (CFO)** cover approved *non-potentially hazardous* foods only. **Fried chicken and hot plated meals are not cottage foods.**  
Source: [CDPH Cottage Food Operations](https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CEH/DFDCS/Pages/FDBPrograms/FoodSafetyProgram/CottageFoodOperations.aspx) (accessed Sep 2026).

Likely paths for this model in LA / SoCal:

| Path | What it enables | Notes |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| **MEHKO** (Microenterprise Home Kitchen Operation) | Cook in home kitchen; sell direct; limited seats / meals | LA County has a MEHKO program; fees reported ~$597 application review + ~$347 annual permit (verify current fees). Excludes some cities (e.g. Pasadena, Long Beach, Vernon often have separate jurisdictions). |
| **Temporary Food Facility (TFF)** | Farmers markets / events | Often required when operating at a market booth; coordinator + county EH. |
| **Mobile Food Facility / cart** | Ongoing street/car sales | Higher bar (commissary, vehicle requirements) — usually later-stage. |
| **Seller's permit (CDTFA)** | Collect sales tax | Free to obtain; needed for taxable food sales in many setups. |
| **Food handler / manager certs** | Required for MEHKO / many permits | Budget time + ~$10–$150 for cards/certs. |

**Action:** Call your **county Environmental Health** department *before* first paid sale. Ask specifically: MEHKO vs TFF vs mobile for “home-cooked plates sold hot at farmers markets and from a vehicle.”

## 8. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Selling hot meat without proper permit | MEHKO/TFF first; no “cottage food” shortcut |
| Chicken food cost spike (wings) | Bias plates toward drumsticks; advertise “2–3 pieces” |
| Soggy cornbread | Separate compartment or wrap; pack greens last |
| Oil cost / quality | Filter; schedule oil change; track $ oil per batch |
| Heat loss in car | Insulated bags + limit open-lid time; back-up chafer at booth |

## 9. Growth engines — Happy Hour, Plate Club, Rewards, TikTok Live, Overnight Ship

**Scope:** Ops and offer design. **Website stays separate** (this plan does not redefine site IA). Full numbers live in `shipping-and-growth-economics.csv` and `deals-economics.csv`.

### 9.1 Overnight shipping (viable primary channel)

Ship **hot or cold overnight** in proper **Uline insulated** packaging before carrier pickup. Packed correctly, the plate holds for overnight transit — this is a standard meal-ship method, not an experiment.

| Path | Pack | Media | When to use |
|------|------|-------|-------------|
| **Hot** | Uline foam shipper (e.g. S-11356-class) | Instant heat packs **or** Uline gels used as heat sinks | Same-day cook → next-day hot arrival; SoCal + eligible overnight ZIPs |
| **Cold / meal-prep** | Foam kit **or** Cool Shield liner + box | 2× 32 oz gels (or 3× 16 oz one-way) | National meal-prep; often best economics on 2–4 packs |

**Full BOM (cost every time):** shipper + media + inner food tray + overnight label + labor minutes.  
**Headline customer ship charge:** **$65** for 1-plate overnight mid-zone (band **$59–$75**; foam/hot often **$69–$75**). Dinner stays **$20** — do not bury freight in the plate price.  
**Process:** correct pack temp → seal → label → **carrier pickup before cutoff**. Discipline makes it work.

**Checklist (not a kill switch):** confirm facility path if MEHKO bars mail-order; interstate PHF usually needs a licensed kitchen — clear it, then sell. Prefer **gel packs** over dry ice (UN1845 / carrier rules if you ever use dry ice).

### 9.2 Happy Hour games

- **$1 spin wheel** during happy hour (engagement “optimal” in-window): use a **house-edge prize table** (EV cost ~**$0.56** → edge ~**+$0.44**). See CSV safe table.
- **Safer default until counsel reviews:** free spin **with plate purchase**, or **trivia → promo code** (lower sweepstakes/gambling risk). Not legal advice.
- Prizes skew toward free drink / small bite / % off — **free full dinner rare**.

### 9.3 Plate Club (subscription)

Half-off plates → **$10** effective on the **$20** dinner. At mid COGS ~**$3.70**, contribution ≈ **$6.30** (63% margin) — still fruitful. Start local pickup; ship members pay freight on top. Churn placeholder **~8%/mo** until real data.

### 9.4 Rewards

**Buy 5 full-price → 6th plate at 75% off ($5).**  
Cycle revenue **$105** vs **$120** full = **12.5%** loyalty cost; average sell **$17.50**; margin still ~**79%**. Loyalty that does not break the plate.

### 9.5 TikTok Live

Limited drops, sold-out energy, optional tip jar. Assume **2–5%** viewer → buyer until measured. Announce **pack/ship cutoffs** live. Hybrid: live drives **50-plate Soul Food Pop** preorders (local) or overnight drops (Phase 2).

### 9.6 Unit stats to remember

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Local pickup $20 vs mid COGS | **+$16.30** contribution |
| Ship fully loaded mid + $65 freight paid | **~$4.53** on $85 total paid |
| $1 spin safe EV | **+$0.44** house |
| Sub plate @ $10 | **+$6.30** |
| Rewards after 6 plates | **12.5%** discount vs full rev |
| Plates to pay ~$120 insulated inventory | **~8** local or **~10** ship orders |

## 10. POWERHOUSE ROADMAP

Execution path — make each phase pay for the next.

### Phase 1 — Soul Food Pop (validate now)

- **Offer:** Fried Chicken Dinner **$20** (2pc chicken, collards, candied sweets, dinner roll).
- **Cap:** **50 plates** per pop — scarcity + quality control (**marketing scarcity** for the flyer; not a physics limit on weekly demand). Full uncapped demand + kitchen-throughput model: `ops-simulation-flywheel-demand.csv` / `ops-simulation-flywheel-README.md`.
- **Channel:** Local SoCal pickup / car / market / event (permit path per §4).
- **Milestones:**
  - Week 1–2: 10–20 test plates; lock recipe + mid COGS ≤ ~$3.70–$4.50.
  - Week 3–4: first public pop **≥25 plates**; hit **50 sold-out** once.
  - Steady: **40–50 plates/week** local before scaling spend.
- **Win condition:** proven **$16+ contribution**/plate and repeat buyers asking for next date.

### Phase 2 — Overnight Uline + TikTok live drops

- Stand up **Uline hot/cold BOM**; buy starter kit (~**$175** est.).
- Sell overnight with **customer-paid freight (~$65 mid-zone)**; 1-plate and **4-meal prep** SKUs.
- TikTok live: limited drops + pop preorders; call cutoffs on stream.
- **Milestones:**
  - First **10 paid ship orders** (pays back insulated inventory).
  - **5–15 ship orders/week** while holding local pops.
  - Live → measurable conversion (track % viewers → paid).
- **Win condition:** ship channel cash-flow positive after freight; no COGS creep.

### Phase 3 — Retention engine (Plate Club + Rewards + Happy Hour)

- Launch **Plate Club** (half-off), **buy-5 get-6th @ $5**, Happy Hour **trivia or counsel-cleared wheel**.
- Keep website separate; use social + live + SMS/Cash App list for offers.
- **Milestones:**
  - **20+ Plate Club members** or equivalent recurring weekly plates.
  - Rewards redemptions tracked; loyalty cost stays ~**12.5%** band.
  - Happy Hour drives incremental plates without negative EV.
- **Win condition:** repeat rate up; less dependence on cold traffic.

### Phase 4 — Brick-and-mortar / fixed location

- Fund lease with **proven** unit economics (not vibes).
- **When to lease (sample gate):**
  - Sustained **≥80–100 plates/week** blended (local + ship + club), **and**
  - **≥8 weeks** of positive contribution after labor, **and**
  - Cash buffer ≥ **3 months** rent + deposits from operating profit.
- Start small (shared kitchen → stall → tiny footprint) before full restaurant buildout.
- **Win condition:** fixed location multiplies Phase 1–3 offers without breaking the $20 plate math.

### Roadmap one-liner

**Pop → Ship + Live → Club/Rewards/HH → Lease.**  
Each phase stays fruitful; overnight packaging is a growth lever, not a science project.

## 11. Marketplaces, Net-30, Social stats & local delivery

**Scope:** Channel fees, trade credit, paid social guardrails, and local courier policy. Full numbers: **`powerhouse-stats.csv`** and the master **`fifi-comprehensive-metrics.csv`** (highlight rows at top). README: `powerhouse-stats-README.md`.

### 11.1 Marketplace & POS fee impact (on a $20 plate, mid COGS $3.70)

| Channel | Fee shape (research 2026) | Approx. contrib after fee + COGS |
|---------|---------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Local cash / pickup | — | **$16.30** (best) |
| Square Online (processing only) | ~2.9–3.3% + $0.30 | **~$15.30–15.40** |
| DoorDash / Uber Eats @ ~25% | % of subtotal | **~$11.30** |
| DoorDash / UE @ 30% | % of subtotal | **~$10.30** |
| DoorDash Drive / Uber Direct | Flat ~$7–$11/drop (CA Drive often ~$9.95) | Keep **$16.30** if customer pays mandatory delivery fee |

DoorDash Marketplace published bands **15 / 25 / 30%**; Uber Eats **20 / 25 / 30%** (2026 cards). Use marketplaces for **acquisition**, not as the profit engine. Instacart is a weak fit for plated meals (grocery supply-side optional). Toast / Square Online = direct POS alternatives (0% marketplace commission).

### 11.2 Local delivery (private courier, mandatory fees, rideshare)

- **Mandatory customer delivery fee:** recommend flat **$12** SoCal (tiers: near ~$8 / mid ~$12 / far ~$18). Covers Drive/Direct cost + buffer.
- **Outsource:** DoorDash Drive (~$6.99–$10.99; CA ~$9.95) or Uber Direct (from ~$7.99) from **your** order channel — not marketplace commission.
- **Private courier / Roadie / bike-car:** quote-based; model **$8–$25**/drop by distance.
- **FLAG:** Passenger **UberX / Lyft** ToS often restrict commercial food/goods delivery. Prefer **Drive / Direct / licensed courier**. Not legal advice.
- **Rule:** In-radius local delivery with fee-through ≫ overnight economics; overnight stays for out-of-radius / national.

### 11.3 Net-30 / business credit (research estimates)

Uline often extends **Net-30** via “Invoice Me” after business review (EIN, biz account; may want trade refs / D&B). Webstaurant may use Credit Key Net-30. Restaurant Depot is typically **pay-at-purchase** membership. Later: Sysco/US Foods trade accounts. Square Capital / Amex Blue Business = working capital products, not classic vendor Net-30.  
**Cash-flow:** deferring ~**$150–$350** of a 50-plate week’s food/supplies for 30 days floats ops — not legal/financial advice.

### 11.4 Social stats guardrail

Live conversion placeholder **2–5%**. Paid TikTok/Meta CPM/CPC bands in the CSV. Target **CPA ≪ $16.30** (soft pause if CPA > ~$12). Content + live frequency and promo budgets are placeholders until measured.

### 11.5 Channel rank (net $)

**Local cash → local delivery + mandatory fee → overnight ship → marketplace @ ~25%.**

## 12. DoorDash premium pricing + bonus sides (and future bottled drinks)

**Rule:** Never match the **$20 pickup** price on DoorDash / Uber Eats / Grubhub. List **higher** (target **$26**, band **$24–$28**) so the ~15–30% marketplace fee does not erase margin.

**Give a lil more on the backend:** every marketplace bag includes a small sweet — **cake slice** or **cinnamon twist** (COGS ~**$0.40–$1.25**, plan mid **~$0.75**). Customers feel the value; you still land near pickup economics.

| Channel | Menu price | Fee (ex. 25%) | Plate COGS | Bonus side | Approx. contribution |
|---------|------------|---------------|------------|------------|----------------------|
| Pickup / pop | $20 | $0 | $3.70 | — | **~$16.30** |
| DoorDash (match $20) | $20 | $5.00 | $3.70 | — | **~$11.30** (weak) |
| DoorDash premium + bonus | $26 | $6.50 | $3.70 | $0.75 | **~$15.05** (strong) |

**Messaging:** “DoorDash exclusive — includes a sweet on the side.” Pickup stays the deal for locals who come through.

**Future — bottle our own beverages:** After Phase 1–2 are stable (sold-out pops + ship/live working), develop a house bottled drink (tea / punch / agua-fresca style). Placeholder retail **~$3–$6**, COGS **~$0.80–$2.50**, attach to local, DD, and ship. Do not let bottling distract from nailing the $20 dinner first.
