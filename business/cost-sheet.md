### Yield **[E]**

Collards cook down sharply. Rule of thumb: **~1 lb raw → ~5–6 oz cooked** (varies with stem trim and cook time).  
**Serving:** 4–6 oz cooked ≈ **0.75–1.2 lb raw** fresh.

### Cost per serving (~5 oz cooked)

| Source | Calc | $/serving |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Fresh retail | ~$2.00/lb raw × ~1.0 lb | **~$2.00** (high) |
| Fresh bulk / smarter buy | ~$1.00–1.40/lb effective | **~$1.00–1.40** |
| Canned 27 oz (~4–5 servings) | $2.72 ÷ 4.5 | **~$0.60** |
| Frozen 12 oz (~2 servings) | $1.54 ÷ 2 | **~$0.77** |

**Planning band for greens:** Low **$0.55** (canned) · Mid **$0.85** (frozen or careful fresh) · High **$1.50** (retail fresh, generous portion) [E]

**Brand note:** Fresh taste matches “home-cooked”; canned is fine for backup or cost control at markets.

## 6. Restaurant Depot vs Walmart (same plate lens)

Assumptions: 0.60 lb drums-heavy chicken, mid greens (frozen or bulk fresh), Jiffy cornbread, $0.27 container + $0.14 utensils.

| Component | Restaurant Depot path | Walmart / grocery path |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| Chicken | $0.99/lb drums → **~$0.59** meat | $1.15–2.10/lb → **~$0.70–1.25** meat |
| Wings if used | $1.87/lb | ~$3.84/lb — **avoid for cost** |
| Greens | Bulk produce aisle / cases | Bunch or canned Glory |
| Cornbread | Case of mix if stocked | Jiffy **$0.72**/box |
| Packaging | Often cheaper cases in-store | Buy via Uline/Webstaurant either way |
| **Illustrative plate COGS** | **~$2.40–2.80** [E] | **~$2.90–3.80** [E] |

**Takeaway:** Depot membership pays off once you buy chicken by the case and hit regular service days. Walmart is fine for Week-1 tests and cornbread/canned greens.

## 7. Suggested sell price

### 3× food-cost target (classic restaurant heuristic)

Using **mid food-only** (chicken+greens+cornbread+seasoning, no pack) ≈ **$2.25**:

| Rule | Price |
|------|-------|
| 3× food cost | **~$6.75** |
| 3× food + packaging (~$2.69) | **~$8.10** |

Those 3× numbers are **too low for LA market reality** and undervalue labor/permits. Treat 3× as a **floor check**, not the sticker price.

### Market-plausible range (LA / SoCal)

| Channel | Plausible plate price | Notes |
|---------|----------------------|--------|
| Soft launch / car pop-up | **$12–14** | Volume + word of mouth |
| Farmers market | **$14–16** | Recommended band |
| Events / trade shows | **$15–18** | Captive crowd |
| Brick-and-mortar comps | Often **~$18–25** | Soul Food Shack / similar LA plates cited in that zone (2026 menus) |

### Recommendation

| | |
|--|--|
| **Suggested start price** | **$15.00** |
| **Acceptable range** | **$12–18** |
| Mid COGS at $15 | **~$2.90 → ~19%** of sales (excellent for food) |
| High COGS stress test at $15 | **~$4.40 → ~29%** (still OK; tighten chicken/wings) |

If you want a clean “3× inclusive” story for investors: price so that **food+pack ≤ 33%** → at mid **$2.90**, minimum sticker ≈ **$8.70**; market still supports **$15**.

## 8. Break-even plates / day (sample)

**Contribution margin** at sample price **$15** and mid variable **$2.90**:

\[
15 - 2.90 = \mathbf{\$12.10}\ \text{per plate}
\]

| Assumed daily fixed costs **[E]** | What it might include | Break-even plates/day |
|----------------------------------|----------------------|------------------------|
| $50 | Gas + light supplies only | **~5** |
| **$100** | Booth fee share + gas + ice/fuel + small labor | **~9** |
| $150 | Higher market fee + helper stipend | **~13** |
| $200 | Strong labor + fees | **~17** |

**Example:** $100 fixed ÷ $12.10 ≈ **8.3 → plan for 9 plates** to break even; **20–30 plates** is a healthy first market day target.

*Fixed costs are illustrative — plug your real booth fee, mileage, and wages.*
